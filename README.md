# Hi, I'm Simon

I'm a technology leader with roots in Linux, networking and getting things to work. I'm SVP of Workflow Engineering at Pax8 and founder of 1310, an independent ISP.

At work, I focus on making AI and automation useful in the everyday running of a business. At home, I put radios on the bins so Home Assistant can tell whether I've taken them out. Both involve rather more testing than you might initially expect.

## Things I've been building

- **[BinRange](https://github.com/simonjgreen/BinRange)**  
  Ultra-wideband radio tags, custom firmware and Home Assistant, all to answer “have you put the bins out?” A fairly substantial solution to a small domestic problem.

- **[Open Observatory](https://github.com/simonjgreen/OpenObservatory)**  
  A Raspberry Pi acoustic observatory for listening to wildlife in the garden. Started with wanting the bird identification app to keep listening after I'd put my phone away.

- **[Sage Coffee](https://github.com/simonjgreen/sageha)**  
  A Home Assistant integration for connected Sage/Breville coffee machines. Power control, temperatures and machine state, alongside the rest of the house.

- **[Kotoba](https://github.com/simonjgreen/trmnl-japanese-vocab)**  
  Japanese vocabulary flashcards for TRMNL, built for my wife. A small project that gets used every day, which is a particularly satisfying outcome.

## What interests me

Reliable infrastructure, home automation, embedded hardware, radio, and understanding how things actually work. I'm also enjoying how much faster AI lets me turn an idea into something I can try, break and improve.

Away from a screen, you'll often find me in the woods.
